## 2. Research assignments — CLOSE A0011's gaps, NOT list more assistants

### A. Source-level comparative audit of the strongest READY management products
Deeply and independently check full official current source/docs/release artifacts for:
1. **Dagu** (queue, admission hooks, REST/CLI, process lifecycle and Windows Job guards) — can its built-in DAG preconditions, failure handling, expression or worker wrappers make strict 'required enforcement failed ⇒ task NEVER executes' *without patching Dagu*? 2. **lowleveldesign Process Governor** — check complete normal command, named-job monitor, nested Job and assignment rights, real identity/flags of job control, crash/forced parent termination, child propagation, quoting, user-session and stdout/cancel/timeout observability. Does a published stable binary/API allow *another product* to submit owned tasks with fail closure without writing new Win32 code? Do not assume normal launch mechanics apply to attached or run-with-token paths.
3. **Windows-native built-ins** — Task Scheduler instance policies and result codes vs fully bounded shared queue, PowerShell7/.NET runspaces/process start constraints, Process Explorer/System Informer for manual operator status, Windows Job APIs/CPU-rate/ETW/PDH.
