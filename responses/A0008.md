## 1. Main finding and scope

The most useful extension to A0007 is a **task-specific capability library**, not another general chatbot-host comparison. This report examines **57 catalog records**, including existing products, family variants, newly explicit candidates and restricted comparators. These are neither 57 new products nor 57 usable/approved integrations. Twenty focused evidence cards develop the most consequential comparisons; all 17 original Applied branches remain represented.

The strongest research additions are: small Persian ASR alternatives with an actual ONNX deployment path; explicit Persian voice/model artifacts; extraction/classification models whose exact language scope differs sharply; callable native raster/vector/media utilities; version-specific automation regressions; and free-service conditions which change whether a ready product can replace custom work.

**Provisional judgments, not architecture decisions:**
- For narrow extraction and classification, compare structured deterministic matching, Persian NLP and encoder specialists before assigning the job to a general chat model.
- For media deliverables, distinguish editing/assembly from generative synthesis. Ready utilities can handle resizing, SVG export, subtitle conversion and lossless cuts without a local generative image/video model.
- A downloadable model, GUI, HTTP endpoint, MCP tool and website account are different capabilities. None implies permission to read a portal or send a client response.
- Free pricing, legal regional eligibility, commercial output rights and confidential-data suitability are separate gates. An UNKNOWN gate prevents activation, not catalog retention.
- No tool is technically preferred because it is installed, and no newcomer receives a novelty bonus.

No on-device command, diagnostic, executable, model download, inference, login, provider signup, billing action, customer-data upload, message, Notion write, pilot or implementation was performed. The sole intended persistent mutation is the new Bridge response.

### Card 01 — Persian normalization: keep raw text, compare Hazm and DadmaTools
