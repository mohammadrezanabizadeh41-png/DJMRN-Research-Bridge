## 6. Twenty focused evidence cards

These are documentary comparisons, not tests or implementation prescriptions. Evidence confidence is attached to the specific claim, not an unconditional product score.

### Card 01 — Persian ASR: streaming Vosk versus neural alternatives

**F:** The [Vosk matrix](https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models) lists small-fa-0.42 at 53 MB with publisher WER 23.4 on CV17 and 14.0 on FLEURS; the 1.6 GB large-fa-0.42 lists 16.7/11.1. Older-numbered 0.42 is listed with better results than 0.5. The general roughly 300 MB small-model memory statement is **P**, not an exact Persian/laptop peak. These models use Vosk/Kaldi assets, not arbitrary ONNX/GGUF.

**F:** [Omnilingual ASR](https://github.com/facebookresearch/omnilingual-asr) offers CTC and decoder families including 300M v2, alongside 7B variants. The source language list includes fas_Arab. Its Python metadata excludes 3.14; fairseq2 and Windows dependency qualification remain unresolved. “1,600+ languages” is not independent Persian quality proof. Limited/unlimited-audio variants must be identified exactly.

**J:** Compare Vosk for narrow streaming vocabulary, A0008 Shenava for a compact Persian ONNX route, and multilingual Whisper for broader language use. None wins merely from parameter count. Human transcription remains adequate for a short critical item.

**U:** Same-corpus revisions, normalization, training overlap, conversational/code-switched quality, exact exported assets and CPU latency. Vosk scores cannot be ranked directly against A0008 PESTE results: corpus version, decoder and normalization differ. No new audio was processed.

### Card 02 — Persian TTS: small non-neural engine versus neural weights

**F:** [eSpeak NG](https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng) documents CLI/library and Windows support. [Its language source](https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng/blob/master/docs/languages.md) explicitly separates fa from fa-latn. A development list does not certify every packaged binary. It is a speech/phoneme engine, not a chat model; GPL3 and component notices apply.

**F:** [MMS fas](https://huggingface.co/facebook/mms-tts-fas) is an explicitly Persian VITS checkpoint, 36.3M metadata, supported in Transformers. CC-BY-NC4 is a substantive commercial-use gate. It is not a permissively licensed replacement for Piper merely because it is smaller.

**J:** eSpeak is a credible simple speech/accessibility alternative; Piper amir remains the more direct existing neural comparison. Authorized human narration is often sufficient for a few client sentences. Intelligibility, pleasantness and numerical pronunciation are separate qualities; no naturalness score is invented.

**U:** Exact current Windows voice packaging, independent Persian listening tests, MMS export identity and any commercial voice license beyond the declared terms. No speech synthesis was executed.

### Card 03 — Hezar: Persian OCR recognizer and ready task library

**F:** [CRNN fa v2](https://huggingface.co/hezarai/crnn-base-fa-v2) declares Apache2, printed/scanned text focus and a preference for short text crops. It documents mixed LTR digits and longer output handling; text detection must precede recognition for an end-to-end page. [Hezar](https://github.com/hezarai/hezar) supplies task wrappers beyond OCR.

### 9.2 Provisional choice rules by job

6. **Ready assistant/editor:** existing and newly researched products can supply editing/tool interfaces; a native engine is not a permission system or a full host. Zed/MNN do not replace the entire assistant design by themselves.
